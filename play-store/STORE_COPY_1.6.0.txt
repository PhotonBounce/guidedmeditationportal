Break free from the habit holding you back — and rewire your mind with daily audio affirmations.

Power of Mind pairs a clean-day tracker with spoken "I am…" affirmations to help you quit vaping, doomscrolling, drinking, and more. Every clean day, your streak grows, your savings climb, and you become a little more free.

NOW IN 8 LANGUAGES
English, Українська (Ukrainian), Русский (Russian), עברית (Hebrew), ייִדיש (Yiddish), Español, Français, and Deutsch. Tap the globe to switch anytime — the whole app, affirmations included, with full right-to-left support for Hebrew and Yiddish.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Clean-day tracker — see exactly how long you've been free, down to the hour, plus the money you've saved.
• Audio affirmations — 7 themed, present-tense "I am…" sets (I Am Free, Craving Crusher, Morning Power, Calm & Steady, Sleep & Release, Strength & Resolve, Worthy & Whole) over calming soundscapes.
• Panic button — the instant a craving hits, ride it out with a craving-crusher affirmation. It always passes.
• Milestones — Day 1, 7, 30, 90, a year — each one lands with a celebration.
• Private by design — your progress stays on your device. No account required.

FREE TO START
Your clean-day tracker, panic button, and the "I Am Free" affirmation set are free (ad-supported). A subscription — just $1.99/year — unlocks every affirmation theme and removes all ads.

HOW IT WORKS
Take a 60-second assessment about your habit and your triggers, and Power of Mind builds you a personalized affirmation path. Listen daily, lean on the panic button when you need it, and watch your clean days add up.

Power of Mind supports behavior change but is not medical advice or a substitute for professional treatment. If you are in crisis, contact local emergency services.
