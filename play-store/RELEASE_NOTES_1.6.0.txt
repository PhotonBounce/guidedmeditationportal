FULL CHANGELOG (internal)
================================================================

MULTILINGUAL (new)
- Per-app language switching via AppCompatDelegate.setApplicationLocales
  (LocaleManager.kt) + res/xml/locales_config.xml for Android 13+ system
  integration, persisted across launches (AppLocalesMetadataHolderService).
- In-app language picker reachable two ways: a 🌐 globe button in the
  dashboard header and a row in Settings.
- 8 languages: en + Ukrainian, Russian, Spanish, French, German (LTR) and
  Hebrew, Yiddish (RTL, in the values-b+he / values-b+yi BCP47 folders).
- Affirmation content moved to string-array resources (affirmations.xml) so
  all 100+ lines and the 7 theme names localize with the app language.
- Every locale carries the full 57-string UI set + 129 affirmation items;
  validated in CI for well-formedness, no missing/duplicate keys, and matching
  array counts.
- Microsite (photon-bounce.com/pom): nav language switcher, all copy
  translated into the same 8 languages, RTL mirroring for Hebrew/Yiddish, and
  the choice remembered in the browser.

CARRIED FROM 1.5.x (all still in this build)
- Google Play Billing 7.1.1 (policy requirement).
- Android 15 edge-to-edge across every screen; modern transitions.
- Removed restricted permissions the app doesn't qualify for
  (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT, USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), forced out
  of the merged manifest via tools:node="remove" and asserted absent in CI.
- Corrected in-app legal links and brand/copy after the rebrand.

VERSIONS
- versionCode 15, versionName 1.6.0
- compileSdk / targetSdk 35, minSdk 21; Billing 7.1.1; AGP 8.6.1

REVIEW NOTES (honest)
- RTL (Hebrew/Yiddish) is compile-verified and the switcher works, but the
  layouts haven't been checked on a physical RTL device — worth a quick look
  before promoting to production.
- Yiddish is AI-translated and leans on loanwords for modern tech terms; a
  native review would refine it. The other languages are high quality.
- Dashboard "days free / best streak / milestone" counters and some secondary
  screens (quiz, insights, calendar) still render in English — a fast follow-up
  can extend coverage to those.
