# SoundPad Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 24, 2026**

SoundPad ("we", "the app") is a sleep sounds and white noise generator
published by the SoundPad team. This policy explains what data we collect,
why, and what choices you have.

## What we collect

**We do not collect any personally identifying information.** We do not ask
for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or files.

The app does collect the following anonymized data:

| Data | Source | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Crash reports | Google Play (automatic) | Identify and fix bugs |
| Advertising ID | AdMob | Show non-personalized ads to free users; can be reset or deleted by you in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads |
| Purchase status | Google Play Billing | Verify your subscription/purchase status to unlock premium features |
| App-session counts | On-device only | Decide when to show the "rate the app" prompt — never leaves your device |

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not have user accounts.
- We do not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.
- We do not transmit your audio preferences to any server.

## Ads

Free users see banner ads provided by Google AdMob. By default these are
non-personalized. AdMob may use your Android Advertising ID — you can
reset it any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

If you upgrade to SoundPad Pro or Ultimate, all ads are removed.

## Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your credit card or
payment details — Google handles all of that. We only receive a token
confirming that you bought the product.

## Children

SoundPad is rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data
from children under 13. The app contains no chat, social, or user-generated
content.

## Your choices

- **Delete the app** at any time — all local data goes with it.
- **Reset your Advertising ID** in Android Settings.
- **Cancel subscriptions** in Google Play → Subscriptions.

## Changes

If we materially change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date
above and post a notice in the app's release notes.

Questions? Email **support@soundpad-app.com**.
