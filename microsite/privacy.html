Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 June 2026

Power of Mind ("the app", "we", "us") is a habit-recovery and affirmations app. We built it to be private by default: your recovery is yours. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

The short version

  • Your progress — habit, quit date, streak, money saved, settings — is stored only on your device.
  • We do not require an account, and we do not sell your personal data.
  • Optional ads and subscriptions are handled by Google, subject to Google's policies.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your preferences and progress locally (in Android's private app storage) so it can show your streak and personalize affirmations. This includes the habit you're quitting, your quit date, daily cost, triggers, your free-text goal, streak history, and app settings. This data never leaves your device through us, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The free tier may show ads served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may process device identifiers and similar information. Where required (EEA, UK, and other regions), the app presents a consent prompt via Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalized advertising. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions and any in-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — Google handles the transaction. Your subscription status is checked through the Google Play Billing service.

Analytics

The app may collect anonymous, aggregated usage events (for example, that an affirmation session started) to improve the experience. These events are not used to identify you personally.

Children

Power of Mind is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can reset advertising consent in the app's settings.
  • You can clear all locally stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings.
  • You can manage or cancel a subscription anytime in Google Play.

Not medical advice

Power of Mind supports behavior change but is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If you are struggling with a substance-use disorder or are in crisis, please contact a qualified professional or local emergency services.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Power of Mind is developed by Photon Bounce (www.photon-bounce.com). Questions about privacy? Email support@photon-bounce.com.
