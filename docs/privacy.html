Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

SoundPad ("we", "the app") is a sleep sounds and white noise generator. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what choices you have.

What we collect

We do not collect any personally identifying information. We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or files.

The app collects the following anonymized data:

DataSourcePurpose
Crash reportsGoogle Play (automatic)Identify and fix bugs
Advertising IDAdMobShow non-personalized ads to free users; resettable in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads
Purchase statusGoogle Play BillingVerify subscription/purchase to unlock premium features
App-session countsOn-device onlyDecide when to show the "rate the app" prompt — never leaves your device

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not have user accounts.
  • We do not request microphone, camera, contacts, or location access.
  • We do not transmit your audio preferences to any server.

Ads

Free users see banner ads from Google AdMob. By default these are non-personalized. AdMob may use your Android Advertising ID — you can reset it any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. If you upgrade to SoundPad Pro or Ultimate, all ads are removed.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your credit card or payment details — Google handles all of that. We only receive a token confirming the product was purchased.

Children

SoundPad is rated for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no chat, social, or user-generated content.

Your choices

  • Delete the app at any time — all local data goes with it.
  • Reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings.
  • Cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Subscriptions.

Changes

If we materially change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and post a notice in the app's release notes.
