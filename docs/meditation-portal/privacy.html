Privacy Policy

App: Guided Meditation Portal  |  Developer: PhotonBounce  |  Last updated: June 24, 2026

We respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the Guided Meditation Portal app ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

1a. Information You Provide

The App does not require account registration. No name, email address, or personal profile is collected by us directly.

1b. Automatically Collected Information

  • Usage data via Google AdMob: If you use the free (ad-supported) tier, Google AdMob may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in the App's Settings screen.
  • Crash reports via Google Play Services: Anonymised crash and performance data may be collected by Google Play's standard Android Vitals service.
  • Purchase records via Google Play Billing: If you purchase the one-time "Premium Unlock" in-app product, the transaction is processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only a token confirming the purchase; we do not see your payment card details.

1c. Data Stored On-Device Only

The following data is stored exclusively on your device in private app storage and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Meditation streak count and session history
  • Alarm time and sound preferences
  • Breathing exercise settings
  • AI companion (Spirit) conversation history
  • All other app settings

2. AI Companion (Spirit)

The Spirit chat feature sends your typed messages to the Claude API (operated by Anthropic, Inc.) to generate meditation guidance responses. Messages are transmitted over HTTPS. Anthropic's own privacy policy governs how they handle API data; by default Anthropic does not use API conversations to train models. No conversation history is stored on our servers.

3. How We Use Information

  • To display ads and fund the free tier (via Google AdMob)
  • To verify premium purchase status (via Google Play Billing)
  • To improve app stability (via Android Vitals)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention

On-device data is retained until you uninstall the App or clear its data. We do not retain personal data on our own servers.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Since we store no personal data on our servers, most requests can be fulfilled by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. For AdMob data, contact Google. For AI conversation data, contact Anthropic.

8. Security

All network communication (AdMob, Billing, AI API) is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. On-device data is protected by Android's app sandbox.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
