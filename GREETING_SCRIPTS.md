# Guided Meditation Portal — Voice Greeting Scripts

Use these scripts to record your custom audio files for the app. Keep the delivery calm, slow, and spacious.

---

## 🎙️ Script 1: Main Greeting / App Welcome
* **When to use**: Welcome screen, home screen entrance, or daily greeting.
* **Script**:
> *"Welcome back to your Portal. Take a slow, deep breath, and let the outside world fade away. Today, select a practice that matches your energy, or ask Spirit to guide your path."*
* **Pacing**: ~15 seconds. Pause slightly after *"Portal"* and *"away"*.

---

## 🎙️ Script 2: Breathing Transition Intro
* **When to use**: Played at the start of Box Breathing to prepare the user.
* **Script**:
> *"Prepare to align your breath. We will follow a box pattern: inhaling, holding, exhaling, and holding. Listen for the gentle chime to guide your holds."*
* **Pacing**: ~12 seconds. Keep the tone steady and grounding.

---

## 🎙️ Script 3: Quick Calm Reset
* **When to use**: Intro to the 60-second Quick Calm reset.
* **Script**:
> *"This is a 60-second space to ground yourself. Let go of what you are carrying, follow the light, and return to center."*
* **Pacing**: ~10 seconds. Focus on a warm, reassuring tone.
