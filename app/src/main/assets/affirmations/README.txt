Drop your spoken affirmation audio files in this folder.

- Supported formats: .mp3, .m4a, .wav, .ogg, .aac
- The FILENAME becomes the affirmation's display title in the app.
  Examples:
    "I Am Free.mp3"          -> "I Am Free"
    "craving_crusher.mp3"    -> "Craving Crusher"
    "morning-power.mp3"      -> "Morning Power"
- Optional: include a habit keyword in the filename (vaping, smoking,
  alcohol, doomscrolling, social) and the app will prefer matching
  tracks for that user's habit.

When at least one audio file is present, the affirmation player plays the
spoken track over the background soundscape. With no files here, the
player falls back to the built-in text affirmations.
